PinyinLyrics

Lyrics with pinyin, floating over any app.

A free Android app that detects the song playing on your phone, finds its lyrics and shows them in a floating window, with pinyin for Chinese and romanization for Japanese and Korean.

What it does

听

New home screen

A "Now playing" card with blurred cover art, the title with pinyin above each character, progress and the current lyric line. Recents and Favourites in tabs; if a song has no lyrics, search for them or paste your own.

♪

Works with your player

Detects the song in Spotify (free tier too: it spots ads and skips the lyrics search for them), YouTube Music, Metrolist and most other players.

🪟

Floating window

Move it, resize it or shrink it to a bubble. Previous, pause and next buttons, plus a mini mode with just the current line.

拼

Pinyin above each character

Pinyin aligned over every character, with tone marks, numbers or none, HSK level colours and a simplified/traditional switch. Practice mode hides the words you already know.

字

Word cards

Tap a Chinese character in the lyrics to see the word with pinyin, meaning (CC-CEDICT), HSK level and a character-by-character breakdown. Copy it or open your dictionary app.

あ

Japanese, Korean & more

Romaji or hiragana with kanji readings, Revised Romanization for Korean, and zhuyin or jyutping (Cantonese) for Chinese. Detected per song.

🔎

Search & lyrics sources

Find lyrics by title or artist and pick the right version. Synced lyrics come from LRCLIB, with unsynced ones from lyrics.ovh as a second source. Paste your own .lrc if neither has them.

👆

Follow along & share

Tap a line to jump to it in the song. Scroll freely: the highlight comes back on its own. Share a line as an image, and save favourites for offline use.

🔒

Private, free & beginner-friendly

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. A short guide on first launch (and in the menu) walks you through the setup. Available in 11 languages.

Frequently asked questions

Is PinyinLyrics free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Which music apps does it work with?

It reads the song that's playing from the media controls Android shows, so it works with most music and video apps that publish the title and artist. Spotify, YouTube Music and Metrolist are common examples.

Does it work with the free version of Spotify?

Yes. It detects the ads and doesn't look up lyrics for them, so it never tries to skip or get around them. The lyrics come back when the song does.

Why does it ask for notification access?

Android shares the currently playing media information through the notification listener permission. PinyinLyrics uses it only to know the title, artist and position of the song. It doesn't read or store the content of your notifications or messages.

Why can't it find the lyrics for some songs?

Synced lyrics come from LRCLIB, an open community service, and when it has none the app tries lyrics.ovh for unsynced text. Not every song is available. You can search by title or artist, pick another version or paste your own lyrics, and the app remembers your choice for that song.

Does it work offline?

Pinyin, romaji and romanization are generated on your phone. Lyrics need an internet connection to download, but songs you save as favourites can be opened without one.

Can I look up a word from the lyrics?

Yes. Tap a Chinese character in the lyrics to open its word card: pinyin, meaning from CC-CEDICT, HSK level and a character-by-character breakdown. You can copy it or open your dictionary app.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not for now. PinyinLyrics is for Android 8.0 and newer.

Which languages does it support?

It shows pinyin for Chinese (simplified and traditional), romaji for Japanese and Revised Romanization for Korean. The app itself is available in 11 languages.
