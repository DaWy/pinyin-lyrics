PinyinLyrics

Lyrics with pinyin, floating over any app.

A free Android app that detects the song playing on your phone, finds its lyrics and shows them in a floating window, with pinyin for Chinese and romanization for Japanese and Korean.

Join the testers group

What it does

🎵

Detects your song

Reads the song playing in your music app and shows its synced lyrics. The current line grows and stays highlighted in real time.

🪟

Floating window

Move it, resize it or shrink it to a bubble. Previous, pause and next buttons without leaving your app.

拼

Pinyin above each character

Pinyin aligned over every character, with tone marks, numbers or none, HSK level colours and a simplified/traditional switch.

注

Practice mode

Hide the pinyin for HSK words you already know and read only what you still need. Also zhuyin and jyutping (Cantonese).

あ

Japanese & Korean

Romaji or hiragana with kanji readings, detected per song, and Revised Romanization for Korean.

👆

Follow along

Tap a line to jump to it in the song. Scroll freely: the highlight comes back on its own, and auto-scroll can be turned off.

🔎

Search & favourites

Find lyrics by title or artist, pick the right version, paste your own .lrc, and save favourites for offline use.

📤

Share & shortcuts

Share a line as an image, adjust the sync offset for every song, and open the window from a Quick Settings tile.

🔒

Private & free

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Available in 11 languages.

Frequently asked questions

Is PinyinLyrics free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Which music apps does it work with?

It reads the song that's playing from the media controls Android shows, so it works with most music and video apps that publish the title and artist. Spotify and YouTube Music are common examples.

Why does it ask for notification access?

Android shares the currently playing media information through the notification listener permission. PinyinLyrics uses it only to know the title, artist and position of the song. It doesn't read or store the content of your notifications or messages.

Why can't it find the lyrics for some songs?

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, an open community service, and not every song is available there. You can search by title or artist and pick another version, and the app remembers your choice for that song.

Does it work offline?

Pinyin, romaji and romanization are generated on your phone. Lyrics need an internet connection to download, but songs you save as favourites can be opened without one.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not for now. PinyinLyrics is for Android 8.0 and newer.

Which languages does it support?

It shows pinyin for Chinese (simplified and traditional), romaji for Japanese and Revised Romanization for Korean. The app itself is available in 11 languages.
